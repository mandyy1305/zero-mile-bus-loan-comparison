4. Items to confirm

1. Loan-to-cost. Assumed 100% financing; IREDA funds up to 80% of EV-fleet cost, banks/PFC typically 70-80%. Confirm promoter margin.
2. Rates are indicative, not firm. SBI = EBLR/MCLR + spread; HDFC/Axis bands target smaller tickets; a Rs 35 cr fleet needs corporate/consortium pricing. Get written quotes.
3. Fixed vs floating. Under calibrated tightening, floating rates may rise. Confirm resets (SBI MCLR/EBLR; PFC 1-yr vs 3-yr reset +25bp; IREDA matrix).
4. Tenure fit. IREDA caps EV-fleet term at 8 years (10-yr only for charging infra); HDFC/Axis/SIDBI products typically 2-7 yrs. A true 10-yr term needs corporate/infra structuring.
5. Fees (not in EMI). Processing up to ~2% (~Rs 70 lakh on Rs 35 cr) + legal/valuation + GST on interest & charges.
6. Security & covenants. IREDA/PFC: mortgage + hypothecation + DSRA + guarantees; banks: collateral & DSCR. Confirm buses (movables) accepted as primary security.
7. Subsidies. Check PM e-Bus Sewa / state EV subsidies / green-finance benefits - they cut the borrowing need directly.
8. Effective dates. PFC w.e.f. 01-Apr-2026; IREDA matrix w.e.f. 01-Oct-2026; SIDBI from published direct-loan PDF. Re-confirm current day.
