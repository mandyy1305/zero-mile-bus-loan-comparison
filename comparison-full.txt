4. ITEMS TO CONFIRM
[1] Loan-to-cost: assumed 100% financing; IREDA funds up to 80% of EV-fleet cost, banks/PFC 70-80%. Confirm promoter margin.
[2] Rates are indicative, not firm: SBI = EBLR/MCLR + spread; HDFC/Axis bands target smaller tickets; a Rs 35 cr fleet needs corporate/consortium pricing. Get written quotes.
[3] Fixed vs floating: under calibrated tightening, floating rates may rise. Confirm resets (SBI MCLR/EBLR; PFC 1-yr vs 3-yr +25bp; IREDA matrix).
[4] Tenure fit: IREDA caps EV-fleet at 8 years (10-yr only for charging infra); HDFC/Axis/SIDBI typically 2-7 yrs. A true 10-yr term needs corporate/infra structuring.
[5] Fees (not in EMI): processing up to ~2% (~Rs 70 lakh on Rs 35 cr) + legal/valuation + GST on interest & charges.
[6] Security & covenants: IREDA/PFC mortgage + hypothecation + DSRA + guarantees; banks collateral & DSCR. Confirm buses (movables) accepted as primary security.
[7] Subsidies: check PM e-Bus Sewa / state EV subsidies / green-finance benefits.
[8] Effective dates: PFC 01-Apr-2026; IREDA matrix 01-Oct-2026; SIDBI published PDF. Re-confirm current day.
